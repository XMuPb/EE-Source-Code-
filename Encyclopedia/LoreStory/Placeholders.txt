============================================================
  EDITABLE ENCYCLOPEDIA — PLACEHOLDERS & FORMATTING GUIDE
  Version 2.1
============================================================

Placeholders work in LoreStory .txt files, Descriptions (Ctrl+E),
and all Lore fields (Backstory, Personality, Goals, etc.).
They resolve to live game data when displayed.

============================================================
  RICH TEXT FORMATTING
============================================================

  Works in both Descriptions (Ctrl+E) and Lore fields.

  ── Text Styles ──
  **bold text**       Bright parchment color
  *italic text*       Warm muted tone
  __underline__       Bright highlighted text
  ~~strikethrough~~   Faded/dimmed text (redacted lore)
  {small}text{/}      Smaller font, muted (footnotes, whispers)

  ── References & Color ──
  [[Cross Ref]]       Golden highlight (like stat labels)
  {#RRGGBB}text{/}   Custom hex color
  {red}text{/}        Named color preset (see below)

  ── Structure ──
  ---                 Paragraph break / spacer
  # Heading Text      Larger bold heading
  > Quote text        Indented quote (inscriptions, dialogue)

  ── Named Colors ──
  {red}text{/}        Blood red
  {blood}text{/}      Dark crimson
  {gold}text{/}       Royal gold
  {amber}text{/}      Warm amber
  {blue}text{/}       Steel blue
  {green}text{/}      Forest green
  {purple}text{/}     Noble purple
  {silver}text{/}     Silver grey
  {white}text{/}      Bright white
  {orange}text{/}     Warm orange
  {teal}text{/}       Sea teal
  {brown}text{/}      Earth brown

  ── Hex Colors ──
  {#CC3333}text{/}    Custom red
  {#D4AF37}text{/}    Custom gold
  {#4A90D9}text{/}    Custom blue
  {#2ECC71}text{/}    Custom green
  {#8E44AD}text{/}    Custom purple

  EXAMPLE:
    # The Fall of Epicrotea
    **The siege** was a *pivotal moment* in the history
    of the realm. [[Lucon]] led his forces against the
    fortress, and the battle claimed {red}two hundred
    lives{/} in a single day.
    > "We shall not yield this city." — {gold}Lucon{/}
    ---
    ~~The original treaty was voided.~~
    {small}Footnote: Some historians dispute this account.{/}
    The aftermath reshaped the political landscape forever.

============================================================
  CROSS-REFERENCE LINKS — [[Name]]
============================================================

  Wrap any name in double brackets for golden highlight.
  Display-only (not clickable). Brackets are stripped.

  EXAMPLES:
    [[Derthert]] marched on [[Pravend]] at dawn.
    The alliance between [[Vlandia]] and [[Sturgia]] held firm.
    [[{name}]] fled from [[{settlement}]] after the siege.

============================================================
  HOW TO USE
============================================================

  1. Write entries in .txt files in the LoreStory/ folder
  2. Separate entries with "---" on its own line
  3. Use placeholders for dynamic content
  4. Use formatting for visual emphasis

  EXAMPLE (Backstory/Lord.txt):

    Born in {settlement}, {name} was the eldest {son} of
    {father}. {He} grew up among the {culture} people and
    at age {age}, {he} commands {troops} soldiers in the
    service of [[{ruler}]]. With {wealth} gold and {renown}
    renown, {his} {clan} stands at tier {tier}.
    ---

  EXAMPLE (Edit Descriptions):
  
    **The Fall of Epicrotea** was a *pivotal moment* in the history of the realm. 
    [[Lucon]] led his forces against the fortress, and the battle claimed 
    {#CC3333}two hundred lives{/} in a single day.
    ---
    The aftermath reshaped the political landscape forever.



  FILE STRUCTURE:
    LoreStory/
    ├── Backstory/General.txt       (all heroes)
    ├── Backstory/Lord.txt          (lords only)
    ├── Backstory/Merchant.txt      (merchants only)
    ├── Personality/General.txt
    ├── Goals/General.txt
    ├── Relationships/General.txt
    ├── RumorsAndSecrets/General.txt
    ├── Founding/General.txt        (clans)
    ├── Territory/General.txt       (clans)
    ├── History/General.txt         (kingdoms)
    ├── Economy/General.txt         (settlements)
    └── Placeholders.txt            (this file)

  PRIORITY: Occupation-specific → General → Template prompts

============================================================
  TIPS
============================================================

  • Use {he}/{his}/{him} for gender-neutral writing
  • Keep entries 3-5 sentences for readability
  • Use [[{name}]] to cross-reference the hero themselves
  • Combine placeholders: "{name} of {clan}, tier {tier}"
  • {enemy} and {friend} are random — different each time
  • 50+ entries per file prevents duplicates in large campaigns
  • Create themed lore packs by replacing all .txt files
